Who we are

About the studio

Smoked Bacon Games is a small indie team that believes games should be fun first. We make games we'd want to play ourselves: charming, a bit silly, and polished until they're crispy.

We work in the open, care a lot about our players, and are always happy to chat with fellow developers, publishers and press.

  • Fun firstIf it isn't fun, it doesn't ship.
  • Small & craftedTight games with lots of personality.
  • Player-lovedWe build alongside our community.
